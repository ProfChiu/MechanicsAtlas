What the dice taught us 🎲

  • 36 equally likely combos, but only 11 totals, so the totals are not equally likely.
  • More combos = more likely. 7 has 6 ways (16.7%); 2 and 12 have just 1 (2.8%).
  • Rolling 7 or more is 21/36 ≈ 58%, a slight favorite.
  • Designer's note: Catan paints 6 and 8 red because they're the hottest numbers on the board (5/36 each).
  • Add a third die and the pyramid softens into a bell curve.
