Watch the open spots. A tile that fits only one place is a gift or a curse. The placement rule turns every draw into a puzzle.

What designers take from this

  • Players build the map. Every game lays out a new garden, so no two games play the same.
  • Matching rules create the puzzle. Each draw asks: where does this fit, and where does it help me most?
  • Closing features gives a clear goal. And every placement can help you or block someone else.
