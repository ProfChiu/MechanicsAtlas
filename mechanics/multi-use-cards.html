What designers take from this

  • Every play is a trade. The use you pick is paid for with the uses printed beside it.
  • Few cards, many decisions. 4 cards with 3 uses each gave 12 options and a real puzzle about order.
  • Balance the bands against each other. If one use is always best, the other two are just decoration.
You'll see this in: Glory to Rome, Twilight Struggle, Race for the Galaxy, Gloomhaven.
