No plan is free. Each card you spend is a building you will never own. That is the whole mechanic: every card in your hand is paying for another one.

What designers take from this

  1. One card, several uses turns every play into a trade. The real price of an action is the best use you gave up.
  2. Cards as money means you pay for good cards with other good cards. A hand of four is a puzzle, not a list.
  3. Fewer cards, more decisions. A small deck stays interesting because each card can mean many things. Balance each use against the others on the same card.
You'll see this in: Glory to Rome, Twilight Struggle, Race for the Galaxy, Gloomhaven.
