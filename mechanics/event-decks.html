What designers take from this

  1. An event deck is the game's storyteller. Each flip changes the world, and the order of the cards sets the pace of the story.
  2. A plain shuffle can ruin the pacing. Storms bunched at the start ended the game in 5 flips; storms bunched at the end made 9 flips feel empty.
  3. Stack the deck to shape tension. One storm per third gives waves of danger; harder piles near the bottom make the waves rise, while every flip still feels like a surprise.
You'll see this in: Pandemic, Arkham Horror, Betrayal at House on the Hill, Dead of Winter.
