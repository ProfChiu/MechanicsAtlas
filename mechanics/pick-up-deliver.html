The greedy cart drove half its trip empty. The chained cart was earning every turn, and it ends the trip already carrying the next order.

The 7-coin order was in both plans. The route around it decides the score.

What designers take from this

  1. Distance is the cost. Every road takes a turn, so an order is only as good as the trip it needs.
  2. Chain your deliveries. The best order is the one on the way to the next one. Chaining nearly doubled the coins in the same 4 turns.
  3. Cargo space is the limit. Two slots force choices about what to carry now and what to leave for later.
