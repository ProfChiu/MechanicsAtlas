Pick-Up & Deliver

One cart, five towns, and the route that pays.

What designers take from this

  • Price the trip, not the cargo. Players should judge an order by coins per turn, so distance is part of every price.
  • Chains are the skill. When drop-off towns are also pickup towns, good players link orders and empty miles disappear.
  • Capacity sets the puzzle. Two slots let a cart carry one order while chasing the next. One more slot changes every route.
