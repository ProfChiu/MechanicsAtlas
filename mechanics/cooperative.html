The village

Six zones. Each burns at 0 to 3 flames. Lines show which zones are neighbours.

Lose: 3 zones burned out.

Win: survive until the wind deck (6 cards) is empty.

Everyone wins, or everyone loses.

The game's turn

  1. Flip a wind card. Add 1 fire to the zone it names.
  2. A zone pushed past 3 flares: it burns out and adds 1 fire to every neighbour.
  3. A neighbour pushed past 3 flares too. That is a chain.

The gap between 2 and 3 is the design. It is why players must work together.

What designers take from this

  • The game is the opponent. It needs its own turn that makes things worse, every time.
  • The threat must outpace any single player. Here the wind adds 3 and one hero removes 2.
  • Unique abilities make players need each other. Reach, power and protection live in different hands.
Designer warning: the quarterback problem. If one player can see everything and decide everything, they start directing the others, who stop playing. Hidden hands, time pressure or private information keep every seat a real decision.
You'll see this in: Pandemic, Forbidden Island, Hanabi, Spirit Island.
