What designers take from this

  1. Shared rolls remove luck differences and downtime. Everyone plays every turn, and nobody can blame the dice.
  2. Permanent marks make each choice matter. Ben's early 11 cost him four boxes for the rest of the game.
  3. The sheet design is the game. Three rows with three rules (ascending, doubles only, storms) create every decision.
