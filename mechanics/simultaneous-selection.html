What made this game work

The best action depends on othersThe Reef paid 2, 8 and 4 on different mornings. Its value was set by the crowd, not the card.
No waitingAll three captains decide at once. Nobody sits through other players' turns.
The collision rule is a dialHere a crowded spot splits the catch. Other games let nobody get it, or give it to whoever is first in turn order.

Ada won with 15 fish while sitting at the Reef only once. Each morning, Ada predicted where the others would go better than they predicted Ada.

What designers take from this

  1. Secret, simultaneous choices turn actions into guesses. The question is not "what is best?" but "what will the others pick?"
  2. Crowding is the core tension. The richest spot paid the least when everyone went there. The empty spot often paid the most.
  3. Collisions need a clear rule. Split, cancel or priority: each choice changes how risky the best action feels.
You'll see this in: Libertalia, Race for the Galaxy, Diplomacy, Colt Express.
