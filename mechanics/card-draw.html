What designers take from this

  1. Decks remember. Drawn cards stay out until a reshuffle, so luck evens out over a deck: every star shows up once per pass.
  2. Deck size, duplicates and reshuffle timing are the dials. A small deck swings fast, extra copies make a card common, an early reshuffle brings it back.
  3. Players who track cards gain an edge. Counting what is gone tells you what is coming.
You'll see this in: Blackjack, Pandemic, Exploding Kittens, Catan Event Cards.
