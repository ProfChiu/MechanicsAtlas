What designers take from this

  1. Each purchase raises the price for the next buyer. Players feel demand directly, and the cheap end of the track goes first.
  2. Turn order becomes money. The same 3 coal cost 10 or 19. Designers rotate or flip turn order so no one keeps the cheap seat.
  3. The refill rate is the dial. Refill less than players buy and prices creep up each round. Refill more and they relax.
You'll see this in: Power Grid, Brass, Food Chain Magnate.
