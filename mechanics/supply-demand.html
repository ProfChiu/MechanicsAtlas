COAL MARKET: PRICE PER COAL

Same 3 coal, three prices

Cy paid more than three times what Ada paid for the same goods. Nothing differed but the turn order.

A designer dial: who buys first?

Turn order is now worth money, so designers use it on purpose.

Leader buys last. The player in first place shops after everyone else and pays the highest prices. A built-in brake on the leader.

Turn the dial the other way (leader buys first) and the rich get richer. Same market, opposite feel.

What designers take from this

  1. Buying moves the price. Each coal bought makes the next one cost more, so the market reacts to what players do.
  2. Turn order becomes money. Going first paid 4; going third paid 13 for the same goods. Designers use that to slow down the leader.
  3. Refills set the pace. How much comes back each round decides whether prices stay high (scarcity) or drift back down.
You'll see this in: Power Grid, Brass, Food Chain Magnate.
