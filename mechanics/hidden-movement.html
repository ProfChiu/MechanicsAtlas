What designers take from this

  1. The hidden player writes, the others deduce. One secret sheet turns a simple board into a game of logic and bluff.
  2. The cloud is the tension. Every hidden move widens the possible places; every search and reveal narrows them.
  3. Tune reveals, map and speed. Those three dials decide whether the hunt feels hopeless, easy, or tense to the end.
