What designers take from this

  • Majorities score, not totals. A region pays whoever leads it. Cubes past the lead earn nothing extra, so every cube is a bet on a contest.
  • The timing of the last piece is the skill. One cube turns a tie into a win. Placing when nobody can answer beats placing early.
  • Second-place payouts soften winner-takes-all. They keep a spread-out player in the game, so commitment wins but doesn't crush.
You'll see this in: El Grande, Risk, Small World, Inis.
