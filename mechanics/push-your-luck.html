What designers take from this

  • Risk grows with every push. Each dive is another 5-in-6 bet, and the bets multiply.
  • Banking is the real decision. Rolling is automatic. Choosing when to stop is the game.
  • Tune two dials to set the tension: how likely a bust is, and how fast the reward grows.
You'll see this in: Can't Stop, Incan Gold, The Quacks of Quedlinburg, Zombie Dice.
