What designers take from this

  1. Goals name shapes, not pieces. Every piece is the same lantern. Its value comes only from where it sits.
  2. Overlap is the skill. One square that serves two patterns does double work. Strong players look for those shared squares.
  3. Scarcity makes overlap matter. With fewer pieces than the goals need, players must stack patterns. Goals that pull apart (corners vs. clusters) add tension.
You'll see this in: Azul, Cascadia, Patchwork, Sagrada.
