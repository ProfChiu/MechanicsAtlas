Rules of the canals

  • On your turn, claim one canal. Fill every segment with your boats.
  • A canal can be claimed once. After that it belongs to its owner.
  • Connect the two villages on your secret goal card to score it.

    What designers take from this

    • Claims are permanent and exclusive. Every link taken is gone for everyone else, so the open board keeps shrinking.
    • Secret goals create quiet races. Plum never meant to block Teal. Two hidden plans just wanted the same canal.
    • Chokepoints are where designers put the tension. Count the shortest paths through a link to see where players will collide.
