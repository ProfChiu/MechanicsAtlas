Three different winners from the same six rounds of play. Scoring does not just count the game; it decides what players chase, and how long everyone stays hopeful.

What designers take from this

  1. Running scores show the leader early. Easy to follow, but players far behind can stop caring halfway through.
  2. End-game bonuses keep hope alive. Bo trailed all game and won on bonuses. Players must be able to track them, or the ending feels random.
  3. Hidden scoring keeps the table guessing. Nobody knew who led until the reveal. Great for suspense, harder for leaders to be targeted.
You'll see this in: Ticket to Ride, Carcassonne, 7 Wonders, Splendor.
