What designers take from this

  1. More dice, better odds, but each extra die adds less than the one before (+23, +14, +10, +7, +4 points). Big pools stop feeling different.
  2. Never a sure thing. Even six dice miss 9% of the time. The pool sets the odds, not the result.
  3. Rerolls are a second lever. One reroll of the misses made 3 dice as strong as 6. Rerolls let you reward skill or spending without growing the pool.
You'll see this in: King of Tokyo, Vampire: The Masquerade (V5), Shadowrun, Yahtzee.
