The Brass Vault

Roll dice equal to your skill.

Every 5 or 6 is a hit.

One hit cracks the lock.

All three miss: 2/3 × 2/3 × 2/3 = 8/27 ≈ 30%
At least one hit: 1 − 8/27 = 19/27 ≈ 70%

What designers take from this

  1. Pool size is the dial. More dice always help, but each die adds less: +23, +14, +10, +7, +4 points.
  2. Never a sure thing. Each die cuts the misses by a third, so they shrink but never reach zero. Six dice still fail 9% of the time.
  3. Rerolls are a second lever. Rerolling every miss is worth exactly double the dice. Price it like that.
You'll see this in: King of Tokyo, Vampire: The Masquerade (V5), Shadowrun, Yahtzee.
