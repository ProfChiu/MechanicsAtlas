What designers take from this

  1. The grid decides what "near" means. Squares with free diagonals make corners too cheap. Hexes keep all six neighbours at the same distance.
  2. Line of sight and cover make position the decision. Two steps turned a blocked shot into a clear one; a crate cut the hit chance in half.
  3. Movement points make terrain matter. When a bog costs 3, the shortest path is no longer the straightest one.
You'll see this in: Chess, Gloomhaven, Descent, Memoir '44.
