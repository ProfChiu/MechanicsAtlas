What designers take from this

  1. A shared budget makes every turn a small puzzle: not "what do I do?" but "what is the best way to spend four?"
  2. The cost menu steers behaviour. Raising Dig from 2 to 3 cut digging from 7 to 4 and more than doubled scouting.
  3. Leftover points and odd costs create tension. A 1-point remainder with nothing worth 1 is a real loss, so players plan around it.
You'll see this in: Pandemic, Tikal, Forbidden Island, Through the Ages.
