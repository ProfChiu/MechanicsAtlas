Why about 4 questions?

1 Qtells apart up to 2
2 Qtells apart up to 4
3 Qtells apart up to 8
4 Qtells apart up to 16 ≥ 12

Each yes/no answer can at best halve the field. 2⁴ = 16, so 4 well-chosen questions always find 1 of 12.

What designers take from this

  • Deduction is a shrinking space of possibilities. Players win by ruling things out, not by luck.
  • Good clues halve the space. A question that splits the field evenly is worth far more than a lucky stab.
  • Designers control how much each clue reveals. Trait counts, question rules and clue strength set the pace of the hunt.
You'll see this in: Clue, Mastermind, Cryptid, Codenames.
