Holding is a bet on time.

What designers take from this

  • A hand is a bank of options. Every card you hold is a choice you have not spent yet.
  • Timing beats raw card quality. Both players had the same cards. When they played them decided the score.
  • Hand limits and game length are the designer's dials. A tighter limit or a shorter game rewards spending. A looser limit or a longer game rewards planning.
