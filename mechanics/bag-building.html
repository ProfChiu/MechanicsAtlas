A bag is a deck that is reshuffled before every draw.
Every round starts from the whole bag, so each purchase changes the odds right away.

What designers take from this

  • The player builds the odds, not the outcome. Adding shards raised the chance on every draw from 30% to 50%, but each draw stayed blind.
  • Junk dilutes. Four ash tokens dropped shards from 50% to 38.9% without removing a single one. Junk is a clean way to make a cost or a penalty hurt.
  • A bag reshuffles every round. New tokens pay off at once, so feedback is fast. Compare a deck, where purchases wait for the next shuffle.
