Deck building

Draw pile→Hand→Discard→Reshuffle
  • A new card goes to the discard pile. It waits until the deck runs out and reshuffles.
  • Every card shows up once per pass through the deck. You know it is coming.

Bag building

Bag→Draw→Back in the bag
  • Everything goes back every round. A new token can come out on the very next draw.
  • No guarantee: the same token can come out twice in a row, or not at all. You control the odds, never the draw.

What designers take from this

  1. Buying changes the odds, not the result. Each Bloom raised the chance of a good draw: 30%, 42%, 50%.
  2. Junk dilutes the bag. Two Weeds pulled the odds back down to 44% without removing a single good token. Forcing junk on players is a quiet attack.
  3. A bag reshuffles every draw. New tokens work at once, but nothing is promised. It feels luckier than a deck, and more forgiving.
You'll see this in: Orléans, The Quacks of Quedlinburg, Dungeon Alliance.
