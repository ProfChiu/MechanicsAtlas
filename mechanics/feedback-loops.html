What designers take from this

  1. Positive loops snowball. When winning pays, a lucky first round grew a 2-point lead into 9. Good for ending a game, harsh on whoever falls behind.
  2. Negative loops pull players together. A small bonus for last place kept the race within 2 points and swapped the lead. Close games stay exciting longer.
  3. Most games use both. An engine that grows (positive) plus a brake on the leader (negative). Tune the balance to set how long the game stays open.
You'll see this in: Monopoly, Power Grid, Catan, Through the Ages.
