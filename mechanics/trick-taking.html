TRUMP!

Four suits, ranks 1 to 9

The rules of a trick

1Lead. The leader plays any card. Its suit is the led suit.
2Follow suit if you can. If you cannot, play any card.
3Highest card of the led suit wins the trick. The winner leads next.
4Trump beats everything. If any flames are played, the highest flame wins.

One card that does not follow suit tells the whole table something true. Good players remember it.

What designers take from this

  • Follow suit is one rule that creates deduction. Every time a player cannot follow, the table learns what is missing from their hand.
  • Trump creates surprise. A 2 of flames can beat a 9. Players save trumps for the moment it hurts most.
  • Tracking played cards is the skill. Knowing which high cards are gone tells you when your cards are safe.
