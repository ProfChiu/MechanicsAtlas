If the game ended at turn 5, Rusher would win 4 to 2.

What designers take from this

  • Early investment pays later. Builder's four scoreless turns bought income that paid out every turn after.
  • Combos make engines snowball. The Workshop paid for each Gear, so one purchase lifted income from 4 to 6.
  • Game length decides whether building is worth it. End at turn 5 and Rusher wins. End at turn 10 and Builder wins 18 to 10.
