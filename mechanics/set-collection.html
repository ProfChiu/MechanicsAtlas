What designers take from this

  1. Escalating rewards create goals. When the next piece is worth more than the last, players commit to a set and plan ahead.
  2. An open market makes collections public. Everyone can see who needs what, so players compete for the same pieces and take cards just to deny them.
  3. Designers tune the curve. Steep curves make big sets the goal and the race fierce. Flat curves make every card the same and the tension disappears.
You'll see this in: Ticket to Ride, Sushi Go!, Splendor, Rummy.
