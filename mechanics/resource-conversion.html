What designers take from this

  1. Conversion chains turn simple goods into big rewards. Four wood sold made 2 points; the same four wood sawn and crafted made 6.
  2. Count actions, not just goods. A long chain only pays if there are enough turns left to finish it. Half-built chains score nothing.
  3. Exchange rates are the balance knobs. One changed rate flipped the best plan. Longer chains must pay more per action, or nobody builds them.
You'll see this in: Agricola, Le Havre, Puerto Rico.
