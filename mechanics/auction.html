In many auction games, coins left at the end also count as points. Every coin spent on the owl is a coin that cannot win the next lot or score later.

What designers take from this

  1. The players set the prices, so the designer does not have to. The table decides what each item is worth.
  2. The auction format changes who wins and what they pay. Open, sealed and falling-price auctions sold one owl three different ways.
  3. Limited money makes every bid a trade-off. Overpaying now leaves you short for the next lot and the final score.
