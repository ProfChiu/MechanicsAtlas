The real prize is the lead

Climb 1Ada 3·3→Bo 4·4→Cy 9·9→Dee 12·12 takes the lead
Climb 2Dee 8-9-10→Bo bomb 2·2·2·2 takes the lead→Bo leads 10: out!
Bo held the lowest cards at the table and still won. The bomb won the lead, and the lead let Bo play the last card freely. Whoever leads chooses the shape, so they can dump cards nobody else could.

What designers take from this

  1. Match the shape, go higher, or pass. One simple rule makes every play a challenge to the next player.
  2. The lead is power. When everyone passes, the top player plays any shape. Saving a strong card to win the lead is the core skill.
  3. Bombs are the spice. A rule that breaks the ladder (four of a kind beats anything) keeps low cards dangerous and comebacks possible.
You'll see this in: Big Two, Tichu, The Great Dalmuti, Scout.
