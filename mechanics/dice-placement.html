Dice Placement

Roll your workers. The number decides where they go.

Press SPACE to roll

Every face has somewhere to go

High dice chase the Tower and Forge. Low dice go to the Low Road. Doubles of anything fit the Market. No roll is a total loss.

Linked to worker placement

Spaces still block each other, like in worker placement. The difference is that the roll limits where each worker may go.

WorkerAny worker fits any open space. Every round looks the same.
DieEach worker has a number. A new roll each round reshapes which spaces you want.

What designers take from this

  1. The roll sets the options, the player picks. Dice decide which spaces are open to you; you decide which of them matters most.
  2. Spaces still block. Red's pair of 4s took the Market and pushed Blue's pair of 5s somewhere else. Turn order matters.
  3. Give every roll a use. A consolation space for low dice turned Blue's 1 into gold and first place next round.
You'll see this in: Kingsburg, Alien Frontiers, Grand Austria Hotel, Troyes.
