Mixed requirements turned Iris's 0 into a 4.
Every face has at least two homes. 2s and 3s have the fewest,
so a designer might add a space that wants them.
Each special space fits about 4 rolls in 10. 30 of 216 rolls fit none of them.
Without Compost, those turns would be a total loss.

What designers take from this

  • Dice are workers with numbers. The roll decides where each die may go, so luck shapes your options, not your score.
  • Mix the requirements. High totals, pairs and low numbers each get their own spaces, so no roll is a total loss.
  • Keep the consolation small. Compost pays 1 point, less than any real space, so a good roll still beats a bad one.
You'll see this in: Kingsburg, Alien Frontiers, Grand Austria Hotel, Troyes.
