What designers take from this

  1. One power, a new plan. Each ability changed which moves were best, so every seat played its own game under the same rules.
  2. Test every power against the base game. Counting turns to a goal showed one power far ahead of the others.
  3. Fix the outlier, keep the flavour. A small limit brought the Merchant back in line without taking away what makes it fun.
You'll see this in: Cosmic Encounter, Pandemic, Root, Small World.
