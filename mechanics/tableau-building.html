    How the game works

    • The game lasts 6 turns. Most points at the end wins.
    • Each turn you get 2 acorns, then may play one card by paying its cost. Unspent acorns are saved.
    • Played cards stay on your table and keep working every turn.
    • Points come only from card abilities. Acorns are worth nothing at the end.

    What designers take from this

    1. A tableau is a personal rulebook. Each card stays and changes the rules for its owner, so no two players end up playing the same game.
    2. A card's value is its effect × the turns left. The Seed Shed on turn 1 returned 5 acorns; the Lantern Post on turn 2 scored 4 points, but only 1 on turn 5.
    3. Order is the real decision. The same six cards scored 10 points in one order and 7 in another. Engines first, triggers early, the big payoff last.
    You'll see this in: Wingspan, Everdell, 7 Wonders, Res Arcana.
