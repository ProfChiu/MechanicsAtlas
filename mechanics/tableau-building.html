    What designers take from this

    1. A tableau is a personal rulebook. Each card stays and changes the rules for its owner, so no two players end up playing the same game.
    2. Early cards pay back longest. A 1-acorn Seed Shed on turn 1 returned 5. Timing matters as much as the card.
    3. Triggers chain. One play on the last turn set off four cards. Designers tune chains so they feel explosive but stay countable.
    You'll see this in: Wingspan, Everdell, 7 Wonders, Res Arcana.
