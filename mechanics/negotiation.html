NON-BINDING

Binding deals

The rules enforce every promise.
  • A promise is part of the trade, paid when due.
  • Deals are safe and quick.
  • Less bluffing, calmer table.
THIS MARKET

Non-binding deals

Only goods handed over now are real.
  • Promises can be broken, like Rue's I.O.U.
  • Trust and reputation become resources.
  • More drama, betrayal and table talk.

What designers take from this

  • A trade needs two yeses. Give players different goods and different needs, so that both sides can gain from the same deal.
  • Players judge a deal against the scoreboard, not just their own gain. The leader gets frozen out, a catch-up system you never had to write.
  • Binding vs non-binding is a dial. Enforce promises for calm, fast trading. Let them break for drama, trust and betrayal.
You'll see this in: Catan, Diplomacy, Bohnanza, Chinatown.
