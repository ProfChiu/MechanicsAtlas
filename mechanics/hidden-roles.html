What designers take from this

  • An informed minority vs an uninformed majority. The Wreckers know every role. The keepers know only their own. That gap is the whole game.
  • Public actions are evidence. Votes, team picks and lamp results are how the majority closes the gap.
  • Tune what each round reveals. Team size, vote rules and how cards are revealed decide how fast suspicion narrows.
