Little blocking, little tension.

What designers take from this

  • Actions are the scarce resource. Each worker is one action, and you never have enough.
  • Blocking is the interaction. No one attacks. You just get there first, and that takes the space from everyone else.
  • Balance spaces against workers. 6 spaces for 6 workers is tight. 10 spaces is loose. Turn order matters more the tighter it gets.
You'll see this in: Stone Age, Lords of Waterdeep, Agricola, Everdell.
